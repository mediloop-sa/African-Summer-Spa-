Our Story

Born from Hoedspruit's
two summers.

Since 1994

Advanced treatments, holistic wellbeing

Located in an area known for its two summers lies African Summer Spa. The spa is aimed at providing guests with advanced treatments focused on holistic wellbeing.

Hoedspruit is known for its breathtaking nature and wildlife — the very thing the spa's creator wanted to showcase and bring into the experience at the spa, delivering unparalleled service in an unforgettable setting.

The spa originated in 1994 to give guests the opportunity to experience not only the natural beauty of the area, but its serene, calming atmosphere. The design of the space allows guests to unwind with a therapeutic treatment, or simply beat the heat by enjoying an afternoon poolside with refreshments.

New Ownership, 2021

Meet Biancé

African Summer Spa changed ownership in 2021. Biancé Janse van Vuuren, the current owner, is a Hoedspruit local who jumped at the opportunity to share her passion and expertise in health and wellness with every guest.

African Summer Spa is the perfect place to escape the hustle and bustle of everyday life, to embrace wellness and ultimate relaxation.
